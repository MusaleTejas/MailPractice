## 💡 The Story Behind MailPractice

In modern engineering, data science, and business environments, **communication is your highest-leverage multiplier**. 

A single well-framed email can save an at-risk client contract, unblock a critical software release, negotiate a promotion, or defuse high-stakes workplace conflict. Conversely, vague, passive, or poorly routed emails lead to misunderstandings, missed deadlines, and lost trust.

### ⚠️ The Generative AI Paradox
Generative AI tools like ChatGPT and Copilot make it tempting to outsource our writing entirely with one click. But when AI writes *for* you:
* Your vocabulary and persuasive instincts slowly atrophy.
* You lose the ability to calibrate diplomatic tone under pressure.
* You miss critical nuances in stakeholder management, escalation, and CC/BCC etiquette.

### 🎯 Our Mission: Flipping the AI Paradigm
**MailPractice was built to solve this exact problem.** Instead of acting as an email generator, MailPractice acts as a **deliberate practice simulator and rigorous AI coach**:

```text
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Realistic Backstory │ ───► │ 2. You Write First      │ ───► │ 3. Instant AI Coaching │
│    & Stakeholder Stakes│      │    (Timed & Anti-Cheat) │      │    (8-Dim + Judgement) │
└────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
```

You are placed into realistic enterprise scenarios with distinct workplace personas, real business stakes, and time constraints. You write independently. Once submitted, our AI evaluation engine dissects your email across **8 writing dimensions**, **stakeholder routing accuracy**, and **executive business judgment** — showing you exactly what worked, where you stumbled, and how to improve.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎓 1. The 5-Tier Progressive Learning Path
Rather than a random assortment of writing prompts, MailPractice guides you through a progressive curriculum designed to systematically build your skills from basic email anatomy to crisis leadership:

```
                         ⭐ LEVEL 5 — MASTER
                         (Enterprise Crisis • Zero Hints • Raw Decision Autonomy)
                                │
                                │ (Avg Score ≥ 85 to Unlock)
                         🔒 LEVEL 4 — EXPERT
                         (C-Suite Directives • Incomplete Info • Trade-offs)
                                │
                                │ (Avg Score ≥ 80 to Unlock)
                         🔒 LEVEL 3 — ADVANCED
                         (Stakeholder Routing • To/Cc/Bcc • Escalations)
                                │
                                │ (Avg Score ≥ 75 to Unlock)
                         🔒 LEVEL 2 — INTERMEDIATE
                         (Structured Paragraphs • Deadlines • BLUF Method)
                                │
                                │ (Avg Score ≥ 70 to Unlock)
                         ✓ LEVEL 1 — BEGINNER
                         (Email Anatomy • Formal Greetings • Clear Purpose)
```

* **Level 1 — Beginner (10 Stages | 15 min limit)**: Introductions, document requests, leave notices with task coverage, 1-on-1 meeting requests, proof-of-effort peer help.
* **Level 2 — Intermediate (12 Stages | 10 min limit)**: Bottom-Line-Up-Front (BLUF) sprint updates, deadline extension requests, customer inquiry de-escalations, 360 feedback.
* **Level 3 — Advanced (14 Stages | 8 min limit)**: Mandatory **`To:` / `+Cc:` / `+Bcc:`** routing, 20% vendor price pushback, P0 outage briefings to CTO, scope creep negotiations.
* **Level 4 — Expert (12 Stages | 7 min limit)**: Executive build-vs-buy AI strategy memos, competing stakeholder demands, board compensation inquiries.
* **Level 5 — Master (The Final Mastery Assessment | 5 min limit)**: A $500k contract cancellation crisis with conflicting engineering vs. sales timelines — zero hints, full diplomatic autonomy.

### 🧠 2. Multi-Dimensional AI Evaluation Engine
Every email you submit is evaluated with corporate-grade precision:

| Dimension | What We Grade |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Grammar Accuracy** | Subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, and structural syntax. |
| **Spelling & Typography** | Zero typos, professional punctuation, and capitalization. |
| **Clarity & BLUF** | Logical flow, immediate bottom-line statement, and signal-to-noise ratio. |
| **Professionalism & Poise** | Diplomatic courtesy, constructive phrasing, and emotional composure. |
| **Email Structure** | Professional salutation, paragraph organization, and executive sign-off. |
| **Vocabulary & Diction** | Replacing weak conversational filler with strong workplace terminology. |
| **Requirement Completion** | Itemized audit verifying every required scenario prompt was met. |
| **Conciseness** | Eliminating fluff and unnecessary corporate jargon. |

---

### 👥 3. Rated Stakeholder Routing & Etiquette (`To:`, `+Cc:`, `+Bcc:`)
Workplace communication isn't just about *what* you say; it's about *who* is in the loop:
* **Interactive `To:` Field**: Type the primary decision-maker based on the left pane's persona profile.
* **`+Cc:` Visibility Etiquette**: Evaluates whether operational collaborators are kept in the loop without causing inbox noise.
* **`+Bcc:` Confidential Oversight**: Evaluates whether legal, HR, or departmental heads are copied for sensitive governance without alerting external recipients.
* **Stakeholder Scorecard (/40)**: Provides dedicated feedback on recipient selection and subject line strength.

---

### 🛡️ 4. Anti-Cheat & English Coherence Guard
To ensure your scores reflect genuine capability:
* **Copy-Paste Disabled**: Blocks clipboard pasting and shortcut keys (`Ctrl+V`) inside the editor to encourage authentic formulation.
* **Live WPM & Typing Tracker**: Real-time writing speed, word count, and character telemetry.
* **Gibberish & Keyboard Mash Filter**: Multi-factor algorithm analyzing dictionary word density, repetition entropy, and phonetic vowel distribution. Submissions containing random filler (e.g. `asdf ;lkj`) are strictly scored `5/100` with clear diagnostic feedback.

---

### 🎭 5. 20+ Curated Scenarios + Dynamic AI Scenario Architect
* **Rich Workplace Backstories**: Every scenario features a realistic trigger event, organizational stakes, and recipient personas.
* **Strategic Decision Mode**: For Advanced and Expert levels, the pre-written "Primary Goal" is hidden, challenging you to deduce the strategy and subject line directly from the backstory.
* **Custom AI Scenario Generator**: Enter any custom workplace situation (e.g., *"Negotiating freelance retainer rates"* or *"Requesting conference leave"*), and the AI instantly generates authentic names, academic/corporate emails, expectations, and multi-sentence backstories.

---

### 🔒 6. Privacy-First Local Architecture
* **Zero Mandatory Account Creation**: Jump straight into writing in 5 seconds without password fatigue or signup drop-offs.
* **Local-First Sandbox**: Your scores, streak counts, drafts, and unlocked achievements are saved privately in your browser's `localStorage`.
* **Stateless Evaluation API**: The backend evaluates drafts in real-time and never stores or leaks private draft content.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Prerequisites
* [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (v18 or newer)
* [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/) or [yarn](https://yarnpkg.com/)
* A free [Groq Cloud API Key](https://console.groq.com/)

### 1. Clone the Repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/MusaleTejas/MailPractice.git
cd MailPractice
```

### 2. Configure Backend Environment
```bash
cd backend
cp .env.example .env
```
Add your Groq API key(s) to `backend/.env`:
```env
PORT=5000
GROQ_API_KEY=your_groq_api_key_here
GROQ_API_KEY_BACKUP=your_backup_groq_api_key_here
```

### 3. Install & Start Backend Server
```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```
Backend API will start on **`http://localhost:5000`**.

### 4. Install & Start Frontend Application
In a separate terminal:
```bash
cd frontend
npm install
npm run dev
```
Open **`http://localhost:5173`** in your browser to begin practicing!
